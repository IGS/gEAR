The cell-cell communication viewer visualizes the output of a DominoSignal run, showing the interactions between cell types, transcription factors (TFs), ligands and receptors.

Dot plots show the geometric mean expression of ligand/receptor pairs across sending/receiving clusters. River plots show the signalling pathway from sending to receiving clusters, either fixed on a single TF, ligand or receptor, or restricted to a custom selection.
