Tools for every question

Browsing and analysis are open to everyone. An account is only needed to upload data and save your work.

Explore

Gene Expression See where one or many genes are expressed, side by side across datasets. Datasets Browse public datasets by organism, tissue and assay. Projection Tool Project gene patterns from one study onto other datasets.

Analyze

Single Cell Workbench Cluster, find markers and annotate cell types, step by step. Comparison Tool Find genes that differ between two conditions or cell types. Single-gene Displays Build bar, violin, UMAP and other plots for one gene. Multi-gene Displays Heatmaps, dot plots and volcano plots for a set of genes. Cell-cell Communication Predict ligand–receptor signaling between cell types.

Manage

Dataset Collections Group datasets into a collection you can view and share in one link. Gene Lists Save, upload and share gene lists to search them all at once. Dataset Uploader Bring your own data and use every tool on it. Free account required.

A free account lets you upload data, save displays and share collections with collaborators.
